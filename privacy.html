Privacy Policy

Effective: August 30, 2026

Finance Planner is a local-first personal finance planning application. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it is stored, and how optional Google Drive and Ask ChatGPT features work.

Short version: Your finance data is stored in your browser. Google Drive backup sends a JSON backup directly to your own Google Drive only after you connect it. Ask ChatGPT creates a limited summary for the section you choose and sends that summary to ChatGPT only after you press Open in ChatGPT. Finance Planner does not operate a server that receives or stores your financial data.

Information you enter

Finance Planner may contain information you choose to enter, including account names and balances, planned income and expenses, categories, goals, sinking funds, forecast settings, and balance-history snapshots. This information is used only to provide the planning, forecasting, backup, and user-requested explanation features shown in the app.

Local browser storage

By default, Finance Planner stores its working data in your browser's local storage on the device and browser you use. The app does not automatically send this data to a developer-operated server.

Google Drive backup

Google Drive backup is optional and starts only after you choose Connect Google Drive and approve Google's authorization screen.

Finance Planner requests the Google Drive scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This scope permits the app to work with files it creates or files you explicitly make available to it; it does not grant the app general access to all files in your Google Drive.

When Drive backup is enabled, Finance Planner may access and use:

  • The Finance Planner backup folder and JSON backup file created for the app.
  • The contents of that backup file so the app can save, compare, and restore your Finance Planner data.
  • File metadata needed to locate the backup and determine whether the browser copy and Drive copy differ.

The backup is stored as JSON in your Google Drive. Finance Planner does not currently add its own password-based encryption layer to that file. Access to the Drive copy is therefore protected by your Google account and Google Drive security.

Google authorization credentials

The browser receives a temporary Google OAuth access token after you authorize the Drive feature. Finance Planner keeps that access token in memory only for the current browser session. The app does not store a Google password, OAuth refresh token, Google client secret, or Drive file ID in local storage or in the Finance Planner backup.

Finance Planner stores only limited backup-management metadata locally: the automatic-backup preference, the hash of the last successfully synchronized backup, and the last successful backup time.

How Google user data is used

Google Drive data is used only to provide the user-facing backup, restore, synchronization-status, and conflict-resolution features that you choose to use. Finance Planner does not use Google Drive data for advertising, profiling, credit decisions, or sale to third parties.

Finance Planner's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Ask ChatGPT

The optional Ask ChatGPT feature is user-initiated. Finance Planner first creates a compact summary from the page or section you selected and shows that summary in a preview dialog. Nothing is sent to ChatGPT merely because the page loads or because an AI button is visible.

If you choose Open in ChatGPT, the displayed summary and prompt are encoded into a ChatGPT link and opened in a new browser tab or app. That selected summary is then provided to ChatGPT so ChatGPT can respond to the question you requested. Because the prompt is placed in the destination URL, it may also appear in browser history or other browser-managed URL records.

Finance Planner does not send the full Finance Planner JSON backup, Google Drive files, Google OAuth tokens, refresh tokens, client secrets, or hidden local-storage contents through the Ask ChatGPT feature. Section-level prompts are intentionally limited to derived values and selected descriptions relevant to that request.

If you prefer not to place the prompt in a URL, you can choose Copy prompt and decide for yourself where and whether to paste it. Use of ChatGPT is subject to OpenAI's applicable terms and privacy practices.

Sharing and selling data

Finance Planner does not sell your personal or financial information. The app does not transmit your Finance Planner data to an analytics provider, advertising network, or developer-operated backend. When you use Google Drive backup, the browser communicates directly with Google's services. When you explicitly use Ask ChatGPT, the selected previewed summary is sent to ChatGPT only for the explanation or analysis you requested.

Your controls and data retention

  • You can use Reset local data or clear the site's browser storage to remove the browser copy.
  • You can delete the Finance Planner backup file or folder directly from your Google Drive.
  • You can disconnect Google Drive from within Finance Planner. You may also revoke the app's Google Account access through your Google Account settings.
  • You can export a JSON backup at any time and keep it wherever you choose.
  • You decide whether to open an Ask ChatGPT prompt or copy it. Finance Planner does not automatically submit prompts to ChatGPT.

Security

Finance Planner is delivered as a static web application over HTTPS through GitHub Pages. Reasonable design measures are used to minimize access, including requesting the narrow drive.file scope, avoiding persistent OAuth credentials, limiting ChatGPT context to the selected summary, and requiring an explicit user action before opening ChatGPT. No web application or storage service can guarantee absolute security, so you should protect access to your device, browser profile, Google account, ChatGPT account, and exported backup files.

Children's privacy

Finance Planner is a general personal finance tool and is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect children's personal information through a developer-operated service.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Finance Planner's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are published.
